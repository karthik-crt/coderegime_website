Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to develop a custom software solution?

Timelines vary based on complexity. A simple web app may take 4–8 weeks, while enterprise-grade solutions can take 4–6 months. We'll provide a detailed timeline after our discovery call.

Do you offer post-launch maintenance and support?

Yes, we offer flexible maintenance packages including bug fixes, performance monitoring, updates, and 24/7 support depending on your plan.

What technologies does your team specialize in?

Our team is proficient in React, Node.js, Python, Flutter, AWS, and more. We select the best stack based on your project needs and scalability goals.
